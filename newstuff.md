NVS
Nick Soto Jr has owned and managed NVS Corporate Services Inc for 21 years. The company operates as a building services contractor providing comprehensive maintenance services to commercial buildings in the federal government, private and public sectors, throughout the U.S. Facility services include janitorial, groundskeeping, mechanical & building maintenance, repairs, and renovations. Nicolas Soto, Jr. was ready to expand his company and better equip his employees with professional training. The BDFof Texas was able to help Nicolas Soto Jr. finance the acquisition of a building to be used as a corporate training site. Presently, staff are trained by Yolanda Soto who is a retired RN with background in infectious diseases. The new facility will house the training team to become responsible for planning, designing, developing, implementing, and evaluating all training for staff located in other parts of the country.
Benefit to the Borrower: The project allows the company to acquire a facility for development of a centralizing training model. Centralized training provides: * Consistency and Standardization * Economies of Scale – better training programs and cost * Legal & Compliance – reduces risk by requiring and providing legal and compliance training requirements to all employees. Currently, all training takes place either in NVS existing warehouse, a rented office space, a borrowed office space, which does not allow for consistency and standardization.
Their new location will create four jobs for the Brownsville community.
