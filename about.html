About Business Development Funds of Texas

Empowering Texas businesses for over 25 years with strategic financing solutions

Our Mission

Business Development Funds of Texas is dedicated to promoting economic development by assisting the growth of businesses throughout the state. We provide accessible, affordable financing solutions that help businesses acquire the assets they need to expand, create jobs, and strengthen their communities.

Who We Are

As a Certified Development Company (CDC), we specialize in the SBA 504 Loan Program, offering long-term, fixed-rate financing for major business investments. Our experienced team works closely with businesses, lenders, and community partners to structure financing solutions that meet unique business needs.

Our Expertise

  • SBA 504 Loan Program administration and processing
  • Commercial real estate financing
  • Equipment and machinery financing
  • Business expansion and growth capital
  • Economic development partnerships
  • Job creation and retention programs

Our Impact

Since our inception, we have helped over 1,200 Texas businesses secure more than $500 million in financing, resulting in the creation of over 15,000 jobs across 250+ Texas communities. We take pride in being a catalyst for economic growth and prosperity throughout the Lone Star State.
