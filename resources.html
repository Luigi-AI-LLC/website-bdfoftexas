Resources & Partnerships

Connecting you with the tools and partners you need to succeed

Our Network of Partners

Business Development Funds of Texas has built strong relationships with financial institutions, economic development organizations, and business support agencies throughout Texas. These partnerships enable us to provide comprehensive support beyond just financing.

Lending Partners

We work with a network of qualified lenders who understand the SBA 504 program and are committed to supporting Texas businesses:

  • Regional and community banks
  • Credit unions
  • Commercial lenders
  • SBA Preferred Lenders

Our relationships with these institutions help streamline the financing process and ensure you get competitive terms.

Economic Development Organizations

We partner with economic development agencies across Texas to support business growth and job creation:

  • Local economic development corporations
  • Chambers of commerce
  • Regional planning commissions
  • Workforce development boards
  • Small Business Development Centers (SBDCs)

Business Resources

Small Business Administration (SBA)

As an SBA Certified Development Company, we work directly with the SBA to administer the 504 Loan Program. The SBA provides valuable resources for small businesses:

  • Business planning and counseling
  • Training and educational programs
  • Government contracting assistance
  • Disaster assistance programs

SCORE

SCORE provides free mentoring and workshops for small business owners. Their experienced volunteers offer guidance on:

  • Business planning and strategy
  • Marketing and sales
  • Financial management
  • Operations and technology

Small Business Development Centers

SBDCs offer free consulting and low-cost training to help businesses start, grow, and succeed:

  • One-on-one business consulting
  • Market research assistance
  • Financial analysis and planning
  • Export assistance

Industry Associations

We maintain relationships with industry associations that can provide specialized support and networking opportunities:

  • Texas Association of Business
  • National Association of Development Companies (NADCO)
  • Texas Economic Development Council
  • Industry-specific trade associations

Professional Services Network

Through our board members and partners, we can connect you with trusted professionals:

Legal Services

Experienced business attorneys for entity formation, contracts, real estate transactions, and regulatory compliance.

Accounting & Tax

CPAs and accounting firms specializing in small business financial management and tax planning.

Commercial Real Estate

Brokers and agents who understand business property needs and can help you find the right location.

Insurance

Commercial insurance specialists for property, liability, and business continuation coverage.

Business Consulting

Management consultants for strategic planning, operations improvement, and growth strategies.

Technology Services

IT consultants and service providers to help modernize and secure your business systems.

Educational Resources

We believe in empowering business owners with knowledge. Here are some valuable resources:

  • SBA Learning Center: Free online courses on starting and managing a business
  • SCORE Webinars: Live and recorded webinars on various business topics
  • Texas Workforce Commission: Resources for hiring, training, and workforce development
  • IRS Small Business Resources: Tax information and tools for small businesses
