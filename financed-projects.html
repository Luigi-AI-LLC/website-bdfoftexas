Financed Projects

Success stories from businesses we've helped grow across Texas

Our Portfolio of Success

Over the past 25 years, Business Development Funds of Texas has helped finance over 1,200 projects across the state. From manufacturing facilities to medical centers, retail establishments to technology companies, we've provided the capital that businesses need to expand, modernize, and create jobs.

Project Types We Finance

Commercial Real Estate

We've financed the purchase, construction, and renovation of commercial properties including:

  • Office buildings and professional centers
  • Retail shopping centers and standalone stores
  • Industrial warehouses and distribution facilities
  • Medical and dental office buildings
  • Hotels and hospitality properties
  • Mixed-use developments

Manufacturing & Industrial

Supporting Texas manufacturers with financing for:

  • Manufacturing facilities and production plants
  • Heavy machinery and equipment
  • Processing and packaging equipment
  • Warehouse automation systems
  • Quality control and testing equipment

Healthcare Facilities

Helping healthcare providers expand their services:

  • Medical and dental clinics
  • Diagnostic imaging centers
  • Outpatient surgery centers
  • Physical therapy and rehabilitation facilities
  • Medical equipment and technology

Hospitality & Service

Supporting the service industry with financing for:

  • Restaurants and food service establishments
  • Hotels and motels
  • Entertainment venues
  • Automotive service centers
  • Professional service facilities

Impact Across Texas

Our financed projects have created lasting economic impact in communities throughout Texas:

  • 15,000+ Jobs Created: Our financing has directly contributed to the creation of thousands of quality jobs across the state
  • 250+ Communities Served: From major metropolitan areas to small rural towns, we've helped businesses in every corner of Texas
  • $500M+ in Capital: We've deployed over half a billion dollars in financing to support Texas business growth
  • Economic Multiplier Effect: Each project creates ripple effects throughout local economies, supporting additional jobs and businesses
