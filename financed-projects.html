Featured Success Stories

Real businesses we've helped grow across Texas through SBA 504 financing.

El Rey Colchon (The Mattress King)

Romulo Lazo and his wife, Elizabeth, have owned and managed El Rey Colchon since July 2018. The company operates as an independent retail bed & mattress store in Rio Grande City.

The Project: The BDFof Texas helped them finance the acquisition of a building and minor tenant improvements to expand their business to a second location in McAllen, TX.

Benefits:

  • Expanded to a second location
  • Moved into a stronger market area with higher growth potential
  • Larger showroom for displaying a broader product line

Impact: Their new location created 2.5 jobs for the McAllen community.

"Me gusto mucho trabajar con el equipo del BDF. Me ayudaron con el proceso totalmente en español y me sentía muy agosto preguntadles todas mis dudas. Yo y mi esposa estamos muy felices de tener una secunda locación y recomendamos que todos los otros negocios pequeños que desean crecer aplican para un prestamos SBA 504. Al final del día logramos nuestro sueño y estamos pagando menos de lo que pagaríamos con el banco."
— Romulo Lazo

NVS Corporate Services Inc

Nick Soto Jr has owned and managed NVS Corporate Services Inc for 21 years. The company provides comprehensive maintenance services to commercial buildings in the federal government, private and public sectors throughout the U.S.

The Project: BDFof Texas helped Nicolas Soto Jr. finance the acquisition of a building to be used as a corporate training site. Staff are trained by Yolanda Soto, a retired RN with background in infectious diseases.

Benefits:

  • Centralized training provides consistency and standardization
  • Economies of Scale – better training programs at lower cost
  • Legal & Compliance training reduces risk for all employees

Impact: Their new location will create four jobs for the Brownsville community.
