# Business Development Funds of Texas

Official website for Business Development Funds of Texas - Promoting economic development by assisting the growth of Texas businesses.

## About

Business Development Funds of Texas is a Certified Development Company (CDC) specializing in the SBA 504 Loan Program. We provide long-term, fixed-rate financing for major business investments including commercial real estate, equipment, and business expansion.

## Website Structure

- **index.html** - Homepage with hero section and service overview
- **about.html** - About us page with board member information
- **contact.html** - Contact form and contact information
- **financed-projects.html** - Portfolio of financed projects
- **resources.html** - Resources and partnerships information
- **sba-504.html** - Detailed information about the SBA 504 Loan Program
